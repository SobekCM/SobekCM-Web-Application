About Introduction to Supply Chain Management

Click here to access the chapter.

 

 

 
 

Introduction to Supply Chain Management provides a brief introduction to supply chain mangment. It will discuss what the supply chain is as well as the many various components that go into it. This course consists of a downloadable textbook chapter with text and real world examples. This course supplements the Introduction to Business OpenStax textbook found here: https://openstax.org/details/books/introduction-business
