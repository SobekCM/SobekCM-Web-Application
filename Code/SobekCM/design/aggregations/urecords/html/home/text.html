About University Records

The University Archives is the institutional repository of records that document the history of Barry University. Archives collects, process, preserves and provides access to records of enduring historical value, from the the founding of the university in 1940 to the present. Collections include official records, reports, publications, correspondence, photographs, audiovisual materials, oral histories, memorabilia and papers of Barry faculty.

Archives promotes and facilitate the use of university records among students, faculty, staff, alumni and to the community at large.
