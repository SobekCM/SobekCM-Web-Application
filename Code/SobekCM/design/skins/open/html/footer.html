OPEN NJ

OpenNJ is a statewide initiative lead and managed by Middlesex College, with support of the Virtual Academic Library Environment of New Jersey. The repository provides a space to share open educational resources and other openly licensed learning objects. Ongoing development made possible by the US Department of Education Open Textbook Pilot Program grant.
