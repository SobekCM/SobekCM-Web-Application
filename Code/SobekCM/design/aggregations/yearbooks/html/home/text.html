Torch and Shield Yearbook Collection

Torch and Shield Yearbook Collection includes yearbooks published by the students of Barry University from 1944 to 1993. From 1964 to 1972 Torch and Shield was not published, other titles were published instead: The sundowner (1964), Postscript (1965), Profiles (1966), Reflections (1967), Pacesetters (1968), Bookends (1969), Sunrise sunset (1970), The morning of your life (1971) and Changes (1972).

In addition to portraits of students, the yearbooks include information about student organizations, athletics, events and activities during a given year. They also document the changing attitudes and culture of students and the history of the college.
