The Open NJ repository allows faculty and students to find Open Educational Resources (OER) created and adopted at participating New Jersey institutions along with materials created through the Open Textbook Collaborative grant. By sharing this common space, OpenNJ enables faculty to share OER, expand their knowledge of adopted OER, and identify OER for their potential adoption. Approved members of the academic community can also submit OER online through the integrated OpenPublishing tool. (learn more )
