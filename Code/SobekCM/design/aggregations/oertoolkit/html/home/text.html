OpenNJ OER Training & Development

A collection of materials to help with the development of Open Educational Resources and to train individuals in understanding and utilizing them.

 

Virtual Academic Library Environment (VALE) sponsored the Open Textbook Network's Train the Trainer workshops on February 28 and March 1, 2019 at Middlesex County College, NJ. The materials from those sessions are held in this collection. Each item has a Creative Commons license, and may be remixed and shared as indicated. This collection includes presentations of NJ OER Summit and other related presentations. 

Guidance from the Office of the Secretary of Higher Education are also provided in this collection.

See the VALE OER initiatives page for more information.
